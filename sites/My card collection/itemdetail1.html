Mewtwo (Shadowless)

$100

This card is in relatively good shape and has been mostly stored in a binder for most of the time I have had it in my collection. The reason why it's priced at that amount is due to being shadowless. Shadowless cards are a subset of error cards in the first printing run of the first Pokemon TCG set that are notable for not having a shadow on the right side of the artwork.
